Income

4010 Retail Sales

Use for revenue from artwork, merchandise, products, and other retail items sold by OAC.

4020 Class / Workshop Revenue

Use for income earned from classes, workshops, instructional events, and similar experiences.

4050 Service Charges / Fees Collected

Use for service charges or other fees collected from customers in addition to the primary sale.

4060 Revenue Withheld for Processing Fees

Used when a selling platform withholds processing fees from revenue before the payout reaches OAC.

4090 Discounts Given

Use for discounts, promotions, or reductions applied to customer sales.

Cost of Goods Sold

5010 COGS - Product Costs

Use for items or materials directly tied to products OAC sells. Examples include merchandise purchased for resale, materials used in retail products, and framing materials used on artwork being sold.

5020 COGS - Class / Workshop / Event Supplies

Use for materials and supplies specifically needed to deliver a class, workshop, or event, such as paint, canvases, craft materials, participant supplies, or event-specific decorations.

5040 COGS - Vendor Payouts - Retail

Use for amounts paid to artists or vendors for their share of retail product sales.

5050 COGS - Teacher Payouts - Classes / Workshops

Use for payments to teachers or instructors for classes and workshops.

5060 COGS - Event Labor

Use for labor costs directly associated with providing a specific event.

Helpful distinction
Product Costs vs. Class / Workshop / Event Supplies
5010 · Product Costs The purchase directly supports or becomes part of a physical product OAC is selling.
5020 · Class / Workshop / Event Supplies The purchase is used to deliver or run a class, workshop, or event.

Selling Expenses

6110 Advertising & Marketing

Use for advertising, promotional materials, paid social media, marketing campaigns, promotional printing, and similar expenses.

General Operating Expenses

6210 Rent & Lease

Use for rent, leases, and business-space rental expenses.

6220 Utilities

Use for electricity, water, gas, and other business utilities.

6230 Internet & Communication Services

Use for internet, phone, communication services, and similar connectivity costs.

6240 Repairs & Maintenance

Use for repairs and routine maintenance of the business space or equipment.

6250 Office & Business Supplies

Use for general supplies needed to operate OAC that are not directly tied to a product, class, workshop, or event.

6260 Software & Web/Cloud Services

Use for software subscriptions, websites, web platforms, cloud services, apps, and other digital tools used to operate the business.

6270 Insurance

Use for business insurance premiums and related insurance costs.

6280 Dues & Subscriptions

Use for membership dues and subscriptions that do not fit under software or another more specific category.

6290 Taxes & Licenses

Use for business licenses, permits, and applicable business taxes or government fees.

6300 Meals & Entertainment

Use for qualifying business meals and entertainment-related expenses.

6310 Bank Charges & Merchant Fees

Use for bank fees, merchant fees, credit card processing charges, transaction fees, and similar payment-processing expenses.

6320 Education & Training

Use for business-related courses, training, education, and professional development.

6330 Travel Expense

Use for qualifying business travel expenses.

6350 Charitable Contributions & Donations

Use for business charitable contributions and donations.

Administrative & Professional Services

6420 Legal & Professional Services

Use for legal and other professional services when a more specific account does not apply.

6422 Accounting & Bookkeeping

Use for accounting, bookkeeping, and related financial recordkeeping services.
