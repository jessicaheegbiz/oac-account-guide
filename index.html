Class / Workshop / Event Supplies

Revenue

Retail Sales

Use for product sales, including artwork, merchandise, and other retail items sold through OAC.

Class / Workshop Revenue

Use for OAC-run onsite classes and workshops held at the shop.

Space Rental Revenue

Use for onsite classes or events where an outside artist or organizer is using OAC's space.

Offsite Event Revenue

Use for OAC events held away from the shop.

Service Charges / Fees Collected

Use for service-related charges that are not part of a standard retail sale, class, workshop, space rental, or offsite event.

Examples include missed monthly co-op fees, rent due after sales, travel charges, hourly work, processing or administrative fees, and other service-related charges.

Direct Costs / Cost of Goods Sold

Class / Workshop / Event Supplies

Use this as the usual category for general art or materials purchased by OAC, including class, workshop, and event supplies.

Product Costs

Use this only when a purchase is clearly tied to products being sold, or when it comes from a vendor identified as consistently related to retail products.

If you're unsure, Class / Workshop / Event Supplies is usually the best starting point.

Vendor Payouts - Retail

Use for payouts to artists or vendors for products sold in the shop.

Teacher Payouts - Classes / Workshops

Use for payments to teachers or artists for onsite classes and workshops.

Offsite Event Labor

Use for contractors or other help paid specifically for OAC events held away from the shop.

Simple Supplies Rule

Most art and material purchases can go to Class / Workshop / Event Supplies.

Usually: Class / Workshop / Event Supplies

Use for general art supplies, workshop materials, class materials, and event materials unless the purchase is clearly retail-specific.

Exception: Product Costs

Use only for purchases clearly tied to products being sold, or for a vendor identified as consistently related to retail product costs.

Selling Expenses

Advertising & Marketing

Use for advertising, promotional materials, paid social media, marketing campaigns, promotional printing, and similar expenses.

Client & Networking Gifts

Use for business gifts related to clients, networking, referrals, or relationship-building.

General Operating Expenses

Rent & Lease

Use for rent, leases, and business-space rental expenses.

Utilities

Use for electricity, water, gas, and other business utilities.

Internet & Communication Services

Use for internet, phone, communication services, and similar connectivity costs.

Repairs & Maintenance

Use for repairs and routine maintenance of the business space or equipment.

Office & Business Supplies

Use for general supplies needed to operate OAC that are not directly tied to a product, class, workshop, or event.

Software & Web/Cloud Services

Use for software subscriptions, websites, web platforms, cloud services, apps, and other digital tools used to operate the business.

Insurance

Use for business insurance premiums and related insurance costs.

Dues & Subscriptions

Use for memberships, dues, and subscriptions that do not fit under software or another more specific category.

Taxes & Licenses

Use for business licenses, permits, and applicable business taxes or government fees.

Meals & Entertainment

Use for qualifying business meals and entertainment-related expenses.

Bank Charges & Merchant Fees

Use for bank fees, merchant fees, credit card processing charges, transaction fees, and similar payment-processing expenses.

Education & Training

Use for business-related courses, training, education, and professional development.

Travel Expense

Use for qualifying business travel expenses.

Interest Expense

Use for interest paid on business loans, financing, or other business debt.

Charitable Contributions & Donations

Use for business charitable contributions and donations.

Administrative & Professional Services

Legal & Professional Services

Use for legal and other professional services when a more specific account does not apply.

Accounting & Bookkeeping

Use for accounting, bookkeeping, and related financial recordkeeping services.
