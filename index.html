Oceanside Art Collective

QuickBooks Account & Categorization Guide

How to use this guide

Use this page as a quick reference for how common Oceanside Art Collective income and expenses are categorized in QuickBooks.

Search for a word such as frames, software, workshop, travel, or advertising to find the most likely category.

Income

4010Retail Sales

Revenue from artwork, merchandise, products, and other retail items sold by OAC.

4020Class / Workshop Revenue

Income from classes, workshops, instructional events, and similar experiences.

4050Service Charges / Fees Collected

Fees or service charges collected from customers in addition to the primary sale.

4060Revenue Withheld for Processing Fees

Used when a selling platform withholds processing fees from revenue before the payout reaches OAC.

4090Discounts Given

Discounts, promotions, or reductions applied to customer sales.

Cost of Goods Sold

5010COGS - Product Costs

Costs directly associated with products being sold. Examples may include merchandise purchased for resale, product materials, framing materials used on artwork for sale, or other costs directly tied to retail products.

5020COGS - Class / Workshop / Event Supplies

Materials and supplies used specifically to provide a class, workshop, or event. Examples include canvases, paint, paper, craft supplies, participant materials, decorations, or other event-specific supplies.

5040COGS - Vendor Payouts - Retail

Amounts paid to artists or vendors for their share of retail product sales.

5050COGS - Teacher Payouts - Classes / Workshops

Payments to teachers or instructors for classes and workshops.

5060COGS - Event Labor

Labor costs directly associated with providing a specific event.

Product Costs vs. Class / Workshop / Event Supplies If the purchase directly supports a physical product being sold, use Product Costs. If it is used to deliver a class, workshop, or event, use Class / Workshop / Event Supplies.

Selling Expenses

6110Advertising & Marketing

Advertising, promotional materials, paid social media, marketing campaigns, printing used for promotion, and similar marketing expenses.

General Operating Expenses

6210Rent & Lease

Rent, leases, and occupancy-related rental expenses.

6220Utilities

Electricity, water, gas, and other business utility expenses.

6230Internet & Communication Services

Internet, phone, communication services, and similar connectivity costs.

6240Repairs & Maintenance

Repairs and routine maintenance of the business space or equipment.

6250Office & Business Supplies

General supplies used to operate the business that are not directly tied to a product, class, workshop, or event.

6260Software & Web/Cloud Services

Software subscriptions, web platforms, cloud services, website tools, and other digital services used to operate the business.

6270Insurance

Business insurance premiums and related insurance costs.

6280Dues & Subscriptions

Membership dues and subscriptions that do not belong under software or another more specific category.

6290Taxes & Licenses

Business licenses, permits, and applicable business taxes or government fees.

6300Meals & Entertainment

Qualifying business meals and entertainment-related expenses.

6310Bank Charges & Merchant Fees

Bank fees, merchant processing fees, transaction fees, credit card processing fees, and similar payment-processing costs.

6320Education & Training

Business-related courses, training, education, and professional development.

6330Travel Expense

Qualifying business travel expenses.

6350Charitable Contributions & Donations

Business charitable contributions and donations.

Administrative & Professional Services

6420Legal & Professional Services

Legal and other professional service expenses that do not have a more specific account.

6422Accounting & Bookkeeping

Accounting, bookkeeping, and related financial recordkeeping services.
